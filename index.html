Privacy Policy – Make It Abroad German Vocabulary Bot

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Make It Abroad provides a German vocabulary learning service through WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

  • Student name and WhatsApp phone number.
  • German language learning level (A1, A2, B1, or B2).
  • Quiz answers, scores, and learning progress.
  • Messages sent to our WhatsApp bot.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to register students, deliver vocabulary quizzes, track learning progress, send reminders, and improve our educational service.

3. WhatsApp Services

Our bot uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. WhatsApp and Meta may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store relevant student information in our application database and take reasonable steps to protect it against unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell student personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the bot, including Meta and hosting providers.

6. Data Retention

We retain student information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet legal obligations.

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Students may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by emailing makeitabroad1@gmail.com.

See our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for individuals eligible to use WhatsApp under its applicable age requirements.
