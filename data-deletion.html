Data Deletion Instructions – Make It Abroad

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Make It Abroad respects your privacy. Students can request deletion of their personal information associated with our German Vocabulary WhatsApp Bot.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to makeitabroad1@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered WhatsApp phone number with country code.
  4. Request deletion of your German Vocabulary Bot account and associated personal information.

What Information Will Be Deleted?

  • Student name and phone number.
  • German learning level.
  • Quiz history and scores.
  • Stored learning progress and related account records.

After verifying your request, we will delete or anonymize relevant information, except information that must be retained for legal or legitimate security purposes.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified requests within 30 days and will contact you if additional information is needed.
